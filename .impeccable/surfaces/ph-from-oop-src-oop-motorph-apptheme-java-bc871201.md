## Direction contract

THESIS: MotorPH becomes one coherent working folio for people, attendance and
payroll. Replace huge fixed forms and disconnected navigation with a stable
workspace and explicit task hierarchy.

OWN-WORLD: Deep forest navigation, warm pale workspace, white work surfaces,
emerald action state, crisp Segoe UI typography, restrained hairlines and simple
consistent line icons. Dense tabular information gets room rather than ornaments.

STORY: An employee sees their personal workspace; HR sees the administrative
scope and current section. Both can find their next action and recover from a
failed operation without losing context.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A narrow persistent forest navigation rail anchors the left.
An identity/scope header and clear page heading sit above the working area.
Tables fill the available width; actions live in a consistent toolbar. Profile
fields become grouped two-column sections. Sign-in uses a forest brand panel
beside a focused white credential form, collapsing gracefully at laptop sizes.

FORM: Motor-service workfolio, grounded candidate3, seed1081122b. The signature
interaction is stable navigation with an explicit selected section and compact
scope switching; form sections reflow rather than shrinking labels. State changes
are immediate or short, with no decorative animation or delayed work access.
